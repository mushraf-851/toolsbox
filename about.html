About ToolsBox

100+ free online tools — calculators, text utilities, QR codes and more. No sign-up, no ads, no tracking. Built for everyone, everywhere.

?? Our Mission

At ToolsBox, we believe that essential online tools should be free, fast, and private. We exist to remove the friction from everyday tasks — whether you're calculating an EMI, converting text case, or generating a QR code. We're committed to providing a hassle-free experience with zero barriers: no sign-ups, no ads, and absolutely no data collection.

Our Vision
To become the most trusted collection of online utilities by offering unlimited free access to high-quality tools that respect your privacy and time.
