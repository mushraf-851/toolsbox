About ToolsBox

100+ free online tools — calculators, text utilities, converters and more. No sign-up, no ads, no tracking. Built for everyone.

Why ToolsBox exists

Most online tools are either filled with intrusive ads, require an account, or charge money for basic functionality. ToolsBox was created to change that — a clean, fast, and completely private collection of everyday utilities that anyone can use without barriers.

Every tool runs directly in your browser. Your data never leaves your device. There are no sign-up forms, no email spam, no hidden costs. Just instant results, whenever you need them.
