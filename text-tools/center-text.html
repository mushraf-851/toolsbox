How to center text for Instagram bio

This tool adds invisible Unicode space characters before each line of text to push it toward the center. Instagram, TikTok and most social media platforms recognise these Unicode characters as valid spacing but display nothing visible, creating the centering effect.

📸 Instagram bio

Instagram bios display on a fixed-width area. Using Hangul Filler (U+3164) characters before each line is the most reliable method. Set line width to 30 characters for Instagram bio — this matches the typical display width.

🐦 Twitter / X profile

Twitter profile bio centers reliably with figure space (U+2007) or braille blank characters. Set line width to 32-35 characters for Twitter. Results may vary by device screen size.

🎵 TikTok bio

TikTok bio centering works with Hangul Filler or Braille Blank. Set line width to 28-32 characters. Test on mobile after pasting as TikTok is primarily a mobile platform.

💡 Tips for best results

Shorter lines center more accurately. Keep each line under 25 characters for the most reliable centering. Test on the actual platform after pasting — different devices show slightly different widths.

Frequently asked questions

Why doesn't my text look centered after pasting?
Centering with invisible characters is approximate — it depends on your device screen width, the platform's font size and the specific characters you used. Try adjusting the "Line width" setting (lower = more left-shifted, higher = more right-shifted). Also try different space character options — some platforms filter certain Unicode characters.
Which Unicode character works best for Instagram?
Hangul Filler (U+3164) is widely considered the most reliable for Instagram bios. It's a valid Unicode character that Instagram recognises but doesn't render visibly. Braille Blank (U+2800) is a good alternative. Normal spaces often get stripped by platforms.
Does centered text work on mobile?
The output looks different on mobile vs desktop because screen widths differ. The centering may appear slightly off-center on devices with different font rendering. Most users check their bio on their own device after pasting — adjust the line width setting until it looks right for your target screen.
Can I center text for WhatsApp or messages?
WhatsApp and SMS don't support Unicode space centering well — they use proportional fonts but strip invisible characters in most cases. For WhatsApp, you can try the Normal space option. For truly centered text in documents, use a word processor or document editor that supports paragraph alignment.
