📖 Complete Guide: Accepting Crypto Payments with a QR Code

Cryptocurrency adoption has grown far beyond trading and investing — it's now a real payment option for freelancers, online stores, content creators, and small businesses worldwide. The biggest barrier to accepting crypto isn't technology, it's friction: wallet addresses are long strings of random characters that are easy to mistype and impossible to remember. A QR code removes that friction entirely, letting a buyer simply open their wallet app, scan, and confirm.

Understanding Payment URIs (BIP21 and Beyond)

Most major cryptocurrencies support a standardized payment URI format that wallets can parse automatically. Bitcoin uses the BIP21 standard: bitcoin:ADDRESS?amount=X&label=Y&message=Z. Ethereum-based wallets use a similar ethereum:ADDRESS@chainId?value=X pattern, while Solana and other chains have their own equivalents. This generator automatically builds the correct URI for whichever network you select, so you don't need to memorise the syntax.

Fixed Amount vs. Open Address QR Codes

There are two common QR code styles. An invoice-style QR encodes a specific amount, ideal for one-time payments where you know exactly what's owed. A donation or tip-jar style QR encodes only the address, letting the sender choose any amount — popular with creators, open-source projects, and street vendors.

Security Best Practices

  • Verify the address character-by-character before publishing — crypto transactions cannot be reversed once confirmed.
  • Use a receiving-only address from a wallet you control, never share a private key or seed phrase.
  • Test scan with your own phone using a real wallet app to confirm the QR opens correctly before printing or publishing at scale.
  • Regenerate a fresh address for each major campaign if your wallet supports HD address generation, for better privacy.
  • Keep the QR static — since this tool runs entirely client-side, no expiry, subscription, or third-party server is involved.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a crypto wallet QR code?+
Select your network (Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, etc.), paste your wallet address, optionally add an amount and label, then click Generate. Download the QR as PNG or SVG and share it anywhere.
What is a BIP21 URI?+
BIP21 is the standard Bitcoin payment URI format: bitcoin:ADDRESS?amount=X&label=Y. Most Bitcoin wallets read this format automatically when scanning a QR code, pre-filling the recipient and amount.
Does the QR code include a fixed amount?+
Only if you enter one. Leave the amount field blank to create an address-only QR code that lets the sender choose any amount, which is common for donations and tip jars.
Which cryptocurrencies are supported?+
This generator supports Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), Solana (SOL), Litecoin (LTC), Dogecoin (DOGE), and USDT on the TRC20 network, with the correct URI scheme automatically applied for each.
Is my wallet address sent to a server?+
No. All QR generation happens locally in your browser using JavaScript. Your wallet address, amount and label never leave your device and are never stored or transmitted.
Can I add my logo to a crypto QR code?+
Yes. Upload a logo image in the Customise panel — it will be placed in the centre of the QR code with automatic high error-correction so the code still scans reliably.
Will scanning this QR code move funds automatically?+
No. Scanning only opens the sender's wallet app with the address (and amount, if set) pre-filled. The sender must still review and confirm the transaction themselves.
