📖 Complete Guide: Link List QR Codes for Creators & Businesses

The "link in bio" problem is real — social media platforms typically allow only one clickable link in a profile. Bio link platforms like Linktree, Beacons and Taplink solved this for digital profiles, but the same problem exists in the physical world. A business card, a product package, a conference poster — each can only practically carry one URL or QR code. A link list QR code solves this by pointing to a single page that contains all your important links, giving anyone who scans access to your entire digital presence in seconds.

Best Free Bio Link Platforms for QR Codes

Linktree is the most recognised platform — free tier includes unlimited links, analytics and customisation. Your URL is linktr.ee/yourname. Beacons.ai offers a more designed, media-rich experience with a free tier. Taplink is popular for e-commerce and service businesses. Carrd.co lets you build a full single-page website as your link hub, with custom domain support on the paid plan.

For maximum control and zero platform dependency, build a simple HTML page and host it free on GitHub Pages (yourusername.github.io) or Netlify. This gives you a permanent URL, complete design control, and no monthly fee — ever.

Choosing the Right Links for Your Bio Page

Research shows that link pages with 5–8 focused links outperform those with 15–20 links in terms of total clicks. Cognitive overload from too many choices reduces action. Structure your links by priority: your most important action first (shop, booking, subscribe), followed by your main social profiles, then secondary resources. Avoid linking to the same destination multiple times with different labels.

Pro Tips for Link List QR Campaigns

  • Use a trackable short URL: Shorten your bio page URL with Bitly before generating the QR — this gives you scan count data separate from the platform's internal analytics.
  • Upload your profile photo as the QR logo: A face or recognisable brand logo at the QR centre dramatically increases scan rates in physical environments.
  • Add context text: Below the QR, print "Scan to see all my links" or "Scan for social + shop". Context increases scans by 25–40% on average.
  • Use consistent branding: Match the QR code colours to your brand palette and bio page theme. Consistency builds trust and increases the likelihood of scanning.
  • Seasonal link rotation: Update your bio page for campaigns, launches and seasons. The QR never changes but the destination always reflects your current priorities.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does a link list QR code work?+
A link list QR code encodes a single URL to a page that displays all your links — like a Linktree or Beacons page. When someone scans the QR, they go to your bio page where they can tap any of your links. It's the most efficient way to share multiple links via a single physical QR code.
What are the best free alternatives to Linktree?+
Top free alternatives: Beacons.ai (best for creators), Taplink (best for e-commerce), Carrd.co (best for custom design), Milkshake (mobile app builder), and GitHub Pages (best for developers who want full control with zero cost). All give you a URL you can use with this QR generator.
Can I put multiple URLs directly into the QR code without a bio page?+
A QR code can only encode one URL. To share multiple links, you need a landing page that lists them. Use the "Preview Links" tab above to add up to 8 links — we'll generate a QR that links to a minimal preview page (note: this requires a static page host to work fully). For best results, use a proper bio page platform.
Does this QR code expire?+
The QR code image never expires. The bio page URL inside it remains valid as long as your Linktree, Beacons, or other bio page is active. You can update the links on your page anytime without changing the QR code or reprinting materials.
How many links should I include in my bio page?+
Research consistently shows 5–8 focused, high-quality links outperform pages with 15+ links. Too many choices cause decision paralysis and reduce total clicks. Structure by priority: most important action first, then social profiles, then secondary resources. Quality over quantity.
Where should I display my link list QR code?+
Best locations: business cards, product packaging, event name badges, conference posters, flyers, storefront displays, restaurant menus, email signatures, YouTube video descriptions, Instagram story highlights (as an image), and any print material where you want to connect people to your full digital presence.
Is my bio page URL stored on your servers?+
No. All QR code generation happens entirely in your browser. Your bio page URL and any link data never leaves your device — never sent to any server, never stored, never tracked.
