📖 Complete Guide: Promoting Your Etsy Shop with a QR Code

Etsy sellers spend hours perfecting product photos, descriptions and listings, but the bridge between offline discovery and online sales is often missing. A customer who admires your handmade jewellery at a craft fair, sees your sticker on a friend's laptop, or reads your name on a packaging insert needs an easy way to find you again. Typing a shop name into a search bar is friction; scanning a QR code is instant.

Shop Link vs. Listing Link — Which Should You Use?

A Shop Link QR sends visitors to your full storefront — ideal for general brand awareness at markets, on business cards, or in your social bio. A Listing Link QR points to one specific product page, which works well for single-item packaging, gift tags, or when promoting a featured item in a flyer or seasonal campaign.

Finding Your Etsy Links

Your shop URL follows the pattern etsy.com/shop/YourShopName, visible in your Shop Manager or by visiting your own storefront while logged out. For a listing, simply open any product page and copy the full URL — it includes a numeric listing ID followed by a readable item slug.

Pro Tips for Etsy Sellers Using QR Codes

  • Match your packaging aesthetic: Use kraft, botanical or handmade colour presets so the QR code feels native to your brand rather than a sterile tech add-on.
  • Add a logo overlay: Placing your shop logo in the centre of the QR builds instant brand recognition before the scan even completes.
  • Use Listing Links for promotions: Running a sale on one item? A listing-specific QR removes the extra step of browsing your full shop.
  • Always test before bulk printing: A single scan check prevents costly reprints of stickers, packaging, or market signage.
  • Pair with a call to action: Text like "Scan to shop our handmade collection" next to the QR significantly increases scan rates versus a bare code.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an Etsy QR code for my shop?+
Choose Shop Link or Listing Link, paste your Etsy shop name or full listing URL, click Generate, then download as PNG or SVG to print or share digitally.
Where do I find my Etsy shop URL?+
Go to your Etsy Shop Manager, click your shop name at the top, and copy the URL from your browser's address bar. It typically looks like etsy.com/shop/YourShopName.
Can I make a QR code for a single product listing?+
Yes. Switch to the Listing Link tab, open the product page on Etsy, copy the full listing URL, and paste it in. The QR code will take shoppers directly to that item.
Does this Etsy QR code expire?+
No. This is a static QR code with the link encoded directly in the image. It works forever with no subscription or server, though it's good practice to update it if you ever change your shop name or retire a listing.
Can I track how many people scan my Etsy QR code?+
This generator creates a static QR code without built-in analytics. For scan tracking, use a URL shortener with analytics before pasting the link in, or check Etsy Shop Stats for traffic source trends.
Where should I display my Etsy QR code?+
Popular spots include craft fair booths, product packaging inserts, business cards, thank-you cards, social media bios, flyers, and your shop's banner image.
Is my Etsy shop data stored on your servers?+
No. All QR code generation happens entirely in your browser. Your shop name and link never leave your device — never sent to any server, never stored, never tracked.
