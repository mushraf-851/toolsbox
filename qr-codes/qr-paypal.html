📖 Complete Guide to PayPal QR Codes

PayPal is one of the most trusted and widely used payment platforms globally, with over 430 million active users. A PayPal QR code allows you to accept payments instantly by scanning a code — no cash, no cards, no typing. Whether you're a freelancer, small business owner, or event organiser, a PayPal QR code eliminates friction and speeds up payment collection.

How PayPal QR Codes Work

A PayPal QR code encodes a link to your PayPal.me profile, email address, or any PayPal payment URL. When scanned with a phone camera or the PayPal app, it opens the PayPal payment page with the recipient and amount (if set) pre-filled. The payer confirms and sends — the funds appear in your account instantly. This works on both iOS and Android devices.

Getting Your PayPal.me Link

Before using this generator, set up your PayPal.me link at paypal.me. Choose a username that represents your brand or name (e.g., paypal.me/YourName). This link is your personalised payment URL. Then, paste your username into this generator — the QR code will direct everyone who scans it to your PayPal.me page.

Why Choose PayPal QR Codes?

  • Faster payments: Customers scan and pay in seconds, reducing cart abandonment and increasing revenue.
  • Multi-currency support: Accept payments in USD, INR, EUR, GBP, and many more — PayPal handles conversion automatically.
  • No setup cost: Unlike card terminals, QR codes are free to generate and display. No monthly fees, no transaction setup costs.
  • Global acceptance: PayPal is available in over 200 countries, making it ideal for international businesses and freelancers.

Pro Tips for Maximising PayPal Payments

  • Set up PayPal.me first — go to paypal.me and create your personal payment link before generating the QR code.
  • Choose the right currency — when setting a fixed amount, select the currency that matches your product or invoice. PayPal converts automatically if the payer's account is in a different currency.
  • Leave amount blank for a general-purpose QR that any payer can use for any amount in any currency.
  • SVG for print — always download SVG for any printed material. Scales to any size without pixelation.
  • Add your business logo — the tool auto-enables Level H error correction to keep the code scannable with a logo overlay.
  • Test scan before bulk printing — verify that the QR opens the correct payment page on both iPhone and Android.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a QR code for my PayPal?+
Choose PayPal.me mode, enter your PayPal.me username, optionally add a fixed amount and select your currency, then click Generate and download. Anyone who scans the QR code is taken directly to your PayPal payment page.
What is PayPal.me?+
PayPal.me is a personal payment link feature from PayPal. Your link looks like paypal.me/YourUsername and lets anyone pay you without needing your email address. Set it up at paypal.me before using this generator.
Can I set a fixed amount in a specific currency?+
Yes. In PayPal.me mode, enter an amount and select your currency from the dropdown. The QR code encodes a link like paypal.me/YourName/50?currency_code=INR — the payer sees ₹50 pre-filled and just confirms.
What currencies are supported?+
This tool supports USD, INR, EUR, GBP, AUD, CAD, JPY, SGD, CHF, NZD, HKD, and SEK. PayPal supports many more — you can also use the Custom URL mode to paste any PayPal link with your preferred currency.
Does this PayPal QR code expire?+
No. This is a static QR code — the PayPal link is encoded directly into the image. It works forever with no subscription, server, or expiry date.
Is my PayPal username stored on your servers?+
No. All QR code generation happens entirely in your browser. Your PayPal username never leaves your device — never sent to any server, never stored, never tracked.
