EPF calculator guide — FY 2024-25

EPF (Employee Provident Fund) is a mandatory retirement savings scheme for salaried employees in India. Both employee and employer contribute 12% of basic salary + DA every month. The accumulated corpus earns interest at 8.25% p.a. (FY 2024-25) and is tax-free on withdrawal after 5 years.

📊 How EPF works

Employee: 12% of Basic+DA goes fully to EPF. Employer: 12% split as 3.67% to EPF + 8.33% to EPS (Employee Pension Scheme, capped at ₹1,250/month based on ₹15,000 salary ceiling). Total EPF = 15.67% of basic salary.

💰 Tax benefits

EPF qualifies under Section 80C (up to ₹1.5L/year). Interest earned is tax-free. Withdrawal after 5 years of service is completely tax-free. VPF (Voluntary PF) lets you contribute more than 12% with same tax benefits.

🏦 Interest calculation

Interest is calculated monthly but credited annually. Rate for FY 2024-25 = 8.25%. Monthly interest = (Opening balance + Contributions) × 8.25% ÷ 12. This makes EPF one of the best risk-free returns available in India.

👴 EPS Pension

EPS (Employee Pension Scheme) provides monthly pension after age 58 with minimum 10 years service. Maximum pension = ₹7,500/month. Employer contributes 8.33% (max ₹1,250/mo) to EPS. Employee does NOT contribute to EPS directly.

Frequently asked questions

What is the EPF interest rate for FY 2024-25?
The EPFO board declared 8.25% per annum for FY 2024-25, ratified by the government. Interest is calculated monthly but credited to your account annually at the end of the financial year. This is one of the highest guaranteed returns on a government-backed savings instrument in India.
Can I contribute more than 12% to EPF?
Yes — this is called VPF (Voluntary Provident Fund). You can contribute up to 100% of your basic salary to VPF. It earns the same 8.25% interest as EPF and gets the same Section 80C tax benefit. However, your employer is NOT required to match the extra contribution above 12%.
When can I withdraw my full EPF balance?
Full withdrawal: after retirement (age 58), or after 2 months of continuous unemployment, or if emigrating abroad. Partial withdrawal for housing, medical, marriage/education is allowed after certain service years. Withdrawal before 5 years of service attracts TDS and is added to taxable income.
Is EPF better than PPF for retirement?
Both are excellent. EPF advantage: employer contributes 3.67%, giving you free money. PPF advantage: flexible contribution (₹500 to ₹1.5L/year), no salary dependency. EPF rate (8.25%) is slightly higher than PPF (7.1%). For salaried employees, EPF + VPF is usually better than EPF + PPF due to the employer matching benefit.
