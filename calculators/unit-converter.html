Frequently asked questions

How do you convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?
°F = (°C × 9/5) + 32. So 0°C = 32°F, 100°C = 212°F, 37°C (body temp) = 98.6°F. To reverse: °C = (°F - 32) × 5/9. Both Kelvin and Rankine are absolute temperature scales starting from absolute zero.
What is the difference between metric and imperial?
Metric (SI) uses powers of 10 — 1 km = 1,000 m = 100,000 cm. Imperial uses historical ratios — 1 mile = 5,280 feet = 63,360 inches. The US primarily uses imperial while most countries use metric. Scientific work almost always uses metric (SI units).
How many kg in a stone?
1 stone = 6.35029 kg = 14 pounds. Stone is primarily used in the UK and Ireland for body weight. To convert your weight: kg ÷ 6.35029 = stones. Example: 70 kg = 11 stone 0.1 pounds (or approximately 11 stone).
