How is a lucky number calculated?

Numerology assigns each letter a number 1–9 using the Pythagorean system. Your name and birthdate produce several core numbers, each revealing a different facet of your life and personality.

Life Path Number

Derived from your full date of birth. Reveals your natural traits, life purpose and the major challenges you will encounter on your journey.

Destiny Number

Calculated from all letters of your full birth name. Represents what you are destined to accomplish and your innate talents.

Soul Urge Number

Derived from the vowels in your name. Reflects your innermost desires, secret motivations and what truly brings you joy.

Master Numbers

11, 22 and 33 are master numbers — not reduced further. They carry amplified energy and represent higher spiritual potential.

Frequently asked questions

How is a lucky number calculated?
Using the Pythagorean system: A=1, B=2... I=9, J=1 and so on. All letter values are summed and reduced to a single digit (unless a master number 11, 22 or 33 appears). For birthdays, all digits of the full date are summed and reduced the same way.
What is a life path number?
The life path number is derived from your full date of birth and is considered the most important number in numerology. It reveals your natural personality traits, life purpose and the opportunities and challenges you are likely to encounter.
What is a destiny number?
The destiny number (also called expression number) is calculated from the full name given at birth. Every letter is converted to its number value, all are summed and reduced. It represents your natural abilities and what you are destined to achieve.
What are master numbers in numerology?
Master numbers are 11, 22 and 33. They are not reduced to a single digit because they carry heightened energy. Number 11 represents intuition and spiritual insight, 22 is the master builder of dreams into reality, and 33 is the master teacher of compassion.
What is a soul urge number?
The soul urge number (or heart's desire number) is found by extracting only the vowels (A, E, I, O, U) from your full birth name, converting them to numbers and reducing. It reveals your inner desires, motivations and what you truly crave at a soul level.
