How is age difference calculated?

The calculator finds the exact difference between two birth dates by comparing years, then adjusting for whether the second person's birthday has occurred yet in the current year. The result is precise to the day.

📅 Exact day count

The difference is expressed in total years, months, weeks and days — accounting for leap years, varying month lengths and whether birthdays have occurred this year.

Frequently asked questions

How do you calculate the age difference between two people?
Subtract the earlier birth date from the later one. Count the full years first, then remaining months, then remaining days. This calculator handles all edge cases including leap years and different month lengths.
What is a significant age difference in a relationship?
There is no universal definition. A commonly cited informal guideline is the half-your-age-plus-seven rule. However, age gap significance depends far more on life stage, values and compatibility than on a number.
What is the half-your-age-plus-seven rule?
The rule states: the youngest acceptable partner age is (your age ÷ 2) + 7. Example: if you are 40, the suggested minimum is 27. It is a social observation, not a scientific or legal rule.
How is age calculated when birthdays haven't occurred yet?
If a person's birthday hasn't yet occurred in the current year, their age is the birth year difference minus one. This calculator uses exact dates so it handles this automatically and correctly.
Can I use this for siblings or parent-child age gaps?
Yes — enter any two birth dates. The tool works for any pair: couples, siblings, parent and child, friends, celebrities, or any two people you're curious about.
